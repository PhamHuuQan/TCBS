TDNOOK KNOWLEDGE BASE — SAMPLE COMPANY HANDBOOK

1. Working Hours & Work Location
- Standard office hours are from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
- Lunch break is from 12:00 PM to 1:30 PM.
- Flexible remote work is permitted up to 2 days per week upon manager approval.

2. Annual Leave & Holidays
- Full-time employees are entitled to 12 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- One additional day of annual leave is accrued for every three years of continuous service.
- Sick leave with doctor's certification is covered according to national labor regulations.

3. Equipment & Information Security
- All employees are provided with a company laptop for work-related purposes.
- Confidential documents, source code, and customer records must never be uploaded to unauthorized public services.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is strictly required on all corporate accounts.
